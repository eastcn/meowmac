# imagegen 提示词

工具：内置 imagegen。图片用于造型和动作研究。

## 单帧造型

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original running cat silhouette concept for MeowMac, a macOS menu bar status animal.
Input image 1 is a reference contact sheet of five frames from another app. Use it ONLY to understand tiny-scale legibility and the idea of a feline gallop. Create a visibly different ORIGINAL cat character, do not trace or reproduce any reference frame, face, tail shape, proportions, or mascot identity.
Primary request: Draw ONE beautiful, unmistakably feline black silhouette in a natural right-facing running gallop, captured in an airborne extended stride. Graceful but approachable domestic cat, balanced small head, two clear pointed ears, short feline muzzle, supple slightly rounded torso, distinct shoulder and hind haunch, four anatomically plausible legs with tiny rounded paws. Front legs reaching forward at different angles, hind legs swept back with a slight bend. Long elegant tail flows back and gently upward, with a soft tip; visibly distinct from the reference. Lively cat anatomy with a flowing spine, not a dog, rabbit, potato, or chibi blob.
Style: exceptionally clean flat black cutout, smooth controlled contour, no interior outlines, at most one small transparent eye cutout if helpful. A professional small monochrome runner icon; restrained, no decorative details. Strong negative space separating the legs so it reads at only 18 points tall.
Composition: one centered cat, pure strict side profile facing right, horizontal body, all ears/paws/tail completely visible, generous transparent padding, width-to-height silhouette about 2.1:1. No sprite sheet, no alternative cats, no labels.
Color: solid black with real transparent background. No grey shading, no gradients, no white background.
Avoid: oversized round head, huge eyes, smile, thick sausagelike limbs, whisker strokes, fur textures, speed streaks, ground, shadows, text, watermark.
```

## 连续帧试验

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: animation key-pose study for an approved original running-cat menu-bar mascot.
Input image is the EXACT approved cat identity and silhouette style. Keep this same cat: identical pointed ears, short muzzle, restrained head size, rounded slim torso, haunch proportions, long flowing tail and pure black fill. Change pose only, do not redesign it.
Create a CLEAN FIVE-FRAME galloping cycle contact sheet on plain white. Exactly five right-facing side-profile cats, arranged in ONE horizontal row of five equal-width cells, no text, no grid lines. Each cat stays completely within its cell with 10% padding. Same physical head/torso/limb lengths and same drawing scale in every cell. Same center of torso and ground baseline; small natural spine and body vertical movement only. Clear feline anatomy with four legs and simple paws.
Chronological poses left to right:
1. Extended airborne stride, matching reference: front legs forward, hind legs backward, supple torso.
2. Forepaws landing below the shoulder and slightly forward, hind legs sweeping inward; body starts to gather.
3. Fully gathered airborne stride: all four legs tucked under belly, spine gently arches, head remains same identity.
4. Hind paws pushing off under the hips, front legs beginning to reach ahead; spine extends again.
5. Rising into the next extended airborne stride, legs unfolding toward pose 1. No duplicate of pose 1, so the sheet can loop.
The tail gently follows body movement without changing length. The leg gaps must remain readable in a tiny monochrome icon. Smooth deliberate contours, pure solid black cutouts, no shading, no texture, no facial additions, no extra limbs, no missing limbs, no stretched neck, no walking poses. White background for review, no transparency needed for this contact sheet. Wide landscape composition.
```
